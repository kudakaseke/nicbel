About Nicbel Investments

Nicbel Investments is a motor vehicle spare parts supplier serving garages, fleet operators, corporates, insurers, NGOs, and individual motorists.

What We Do

We supply complete motor vehicle spare parts solutions for passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles, light trucks, utility vehicles, and fleets.

Our range includes genuine OEM parts, quality aftermarket alternatives, fast moving service parts, and critical mechanical and safety components.

Mission

To deliver reliable, cost effective and timely motor vehicle spare parts, while offering expert advice that enables our clients to operate efficiently and safely.

OUR VISION

To become Zimbabwe’s most trusted motor vehicle spare parts partner, recognized for operational excellency, integrity and long-term client partnerships.

Values
  • Integrity - honest dealings, transparent pricing
  • Quality first - no compromise on safety-critical components
  • Customer centricity - solutions tailored to client needs
  • Accountability - we stand by every part we supply
  • Efficiency - speed, accuracy and reliability
