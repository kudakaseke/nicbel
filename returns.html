Returns Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

At Nicbel Motor Spares, we are committed to supplying quality motor spares, engines, and automotive components. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, we provide a fair returns process subject to the terms below.

Voluntary returns and statutory rights

The seven-day window and exclusions below apply to voluntary returns of non-defective goods. They do not remove applicable rights for defective, unsafe or incorrectly supplied goods under Zimbabwean law. See Terms and Conditions, section 9 for the separate statutory remedy.

7-Day Inspection Period

Customers have 7 days from the date of delivery or collection to inspect purchased items and report any issues.

Products must remain:

  • Uninstalled
  • Unused
  • In the same condition as received

Once an engine or spare part has been installed, fitted, modified, or used, it may no longer qualify for return unless covered under an applicable warranty.

Return Eligibility

To qualify for a return, the following conditions must be met:

  • Product must be unused and uninstalled
  • Item must be returned in its original condition
  • Original packaging, accessories, and components must be included
  • Proof of purchase or invoice must be provided
  • Return authorisation must be obtained before returning goods
  • Product must be properly packaged for transport

Exclusions for Voluntary Returns

Voluntary change-of-mind returns are not accepted under the following circumstances, subject to applicable statutory rights:

  • The engine or spare part has been installed or used
  • More than 7 days have passed since delivery or collection
  • Product has been tampered with, altered, or damaged by the customer
  • Electrical components or sensors have been fitted or tested
  • Special-order or customer-requested parts
  • Damage caused by improper handling, storage, or installation
  • Clearance or "as is" items unless defective upon delivery

How to Return an Item

1. Contact Our Team

For a voluntary return, contact Nicbel Motor Spares within 7 days of receiving your order to request a Return Authorisation (RA) number. Report a defect promptly; applicable statutory claims are not limited to this seven-day window.

Email: sales@nicbel.org

2. Prepare the Product

Securely package the item to prevent damage during transport. Original packaging should be used where possible.

3. Return or Arrange Collection

Our team will advise whether the item should be delivered to our premises or collected through an arranged courier service. The RA number must accompany the returned item.

4. Inspection and Refund

Returned products will be inspected within a reasonable period after receipt. Approved refunds or exchanges will be processed after inspection confirmation.

Return Shipping Costs

Incorrect Item Supplied

If we supplied the incorrect engine or spare part, we will cover the reasonable return shipping costs and arrange either:

  • A replacement item, or
  • A full refund

Change of Mind

For change-of-mind returns:

  • The customer is responsible for return shipping costs
  • A restocking fee may apply where necessary

Refund Methods

Approved refunds are processed using the original payment method where possible. Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider or banking institution.

Damaged During Delivery

If your order arrives damaged:

  • Notify the delivery driver immediately where possible
  • Take clear photographs of the damage
  • Contact us as soon as possible after delivery

We will work with the courier or freight provider to resolve the issue and arrange a replacement or refund where appropriate.

Warranty Claims

Products covered under warranty will be handled according to the applicable supplier or manufacturer warranty terms. Warranty claims may require inspection before approval.
